# Attribution and distribution

The feature implementation and evaluator were written for this study. The directed lag-one dependence statistic is adapted from [Bennett, Cucuringu and Reinert (2022)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.08283). Executed comparison formulas appear in `research/tested_controls.md`.

This package includes the six evaluated variants, executed controls and diagnostics, aggregate measured results, code, synthetic software fixtures, and relevant provenance. Course inputs, raw vendor prices, detailed price-bearing ledgers, downloaded third-party code and paper PDFs are excluded. Dependencies retain their respective licenses; no new license is granted for third-party work.

The public-facing report and package verifier are presentation derivatives. Model, acquisition, outcome, account and selection modules, numeric metrics, and original freeze records are unchanged. `release/publication_derivation.json` records the exact relationship, including exposure-record identity/citation redactions that retain dates and research facts. Current distribution status is in `RELEASE_STATUS.json`; historical research records retain the status at their creation.
