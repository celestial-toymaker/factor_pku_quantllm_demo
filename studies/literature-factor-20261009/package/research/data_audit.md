# Data audit and information boundaries

The preserved course cache contains 2,668,723 rows for 819 symbols, September 24, 2007–January 10, 2024, and 995 dated membership intervals. Membership breadth is approximately 300, not 819. Duplicate date/symbol rows: zero. The 90,890 missing VWAP observations coincide with absent trading activity; no VWAP imputation is used. Prices are vendor-adjusted and volumes are not raw executable shares. Embedded Arrow buffers were extracted with pickle opcode inspection; pickle objects, reducers and downloaded formula expressions were never executed.

The independent BaoStock supplement requested January 2017–December 2023 for all cached symbols. It yielded 1,295,289 raw daily bars for 806 nonempty histories, with zero missing validation bars for cached members. Thirteen histories are empty in that interval and are explicitly retained in the acquisition manifest. The raw daily fields are CNY/share prices, share volume, CNY amount, `preclose`, `tradestatus` and `isST`, with `adjustflag=3`. Active main-board amount/volume prices lie within the daily range. Duplicate and invalid active OHLC counts are zero. Traded CNY amounts agree with the cache: median relative discrepancy about 1e-10; maximum about 1.45e-7.

Historical corporate-action accounting uses 3,933 normalized dividend events, including two issuer-confirmed Moutai special dividends missing from the primary dividend response. Eighteen optional rights events reconcile remaining large exchange reference resets. Optional subscriptions are consistently declined. Reconciled warnings include different theoretical ex-reference conventions when repurchased shares are excluded: cash per entitled share is booked from the announcement, and exchange preclose sets daily limits. The machine audit preserves every warning; no warning is silently converted into a free total return.

The cache/raw feature-return comparison has a median absolute log-return difference of 0.00000186 and a 99th percentile of 0.00002645. There are 148 differences above 0.001, of which 46 concern contemporaneous main-board members; their largest is 0.004513. Quote-reference rounding and adjustment conventions differ, so the final provider normalization is an explicit measurement limitation. The largest all-symbol difference (600733 on 2018-09-27) is a nonmember restructuring/resumption observation; the network's adjacent traded-session mask removes its interrupted endpoint. This is an input audit, not a candidate return screen. No raw price level or adjusted share volume is used as executable cash economics.

The eligible validation universe has 243–280 stocks (median 266) after dated membership, main-board, 252-observation, CNY20m lagged-median amount and known signal-day trading/ST filters; no validation session falls below 200. All exclusions follow signal-time fields. Newly listed observations and suspended endpoints remain unavailable. Current security-master listing/delisting dates are an audit aid, never used to make a past name disappear. Missing held-stock marks carry the last mark with explicit stale flags; neither zero loss nor a fabricated liquidation is assigned. Full terminal-loss evidence is not inferred from an absent bar.

Prior-year industry snapshots are queried before each validation year. All provider update dates precede the requested date and the scoring year. The 2023 snapshot's latest update is August 2022, illustrating genuine staleness; categories remain stale rather than being backfilled from current labels. Sector coverage is measured in the candidate artifact. The provider does not supply an auditable history of subsequent corrections to archived records. These are dated provider reconstructions, not independently timestamped original database backups.

The feature decision occurs after close; raw status is known for that signal date. Membership is joined on dated intervals. The account checks execution-day status, limits and liquidity only as fill restrictions. Market calendars preserve holidays and missing endpoints. Outcomes crossing split boundaries are purged. Exact final access is gated by `configs/finalist.json` and a single-attempt marker; September prices have not been requested during this preflight.

BaoStock was investigated first and successfully accessed anonymously. Its installed public client supplies the query interface; provider web documentation had incomplete/empty responses during review. A GitHub downloader mirror was identified as third party and is not treated as a provider license. No credential was taken from student code. Data redistribution rights were not established, so all raw panels, source snapshots and third-party PDFs remain outside the archive. Release authorization remains false.

Machine evidence: `data/data_manifest.json`, both fingerprint manifests, `results/history_data_audit.json`, `results/reconciliation_and_breadth.json`, and the private per-request acquisition log. Dates and hashes establish the captured bytes; they do not establish an untouched holdout. The exposure ledger records earlier student coverage through September 23, 2026.
