# Supplementary primary-source fee confirmation

During packaging, [HKEX circular CT/037/22, 28 April 2022](https://www.hkex.com.hk/-/media/HKEX-Market/Services/Circulars-and-Notices/Participant-and-Members-Circulars/SEHK/2022/CT03722B.pdf), page 1, confirmed that the mainland Shanghai/Shenzhen A-share transfer charge changed from 0.002% to 0.001% of consideration on each side, effective 29 April 2022. It states that the revision also applies to Stock Connect. This corroborates the frozen mainland fee schedule; no additional Hong Kong intermediary fees are modeled for the domestic account.

The [SSE investor service page](https://one.sse.com.cn/onething/gptz/) also identifies the current transfer charge and explains the relationship of exchange/regulatory charges to brokerage commission. The frozen commission and slippage are research assumptions, not personalized broker terms.

The HKEX two-page PDF received an all-page visual overview followed by full-size inspection of page 1. Its hash and reading record are supplied; the PDF remains private. This evidence was recovered after the finalist freeze and makes no change to code, assumptions, ranking or outcomes. The original frozen method's description of its then-available secondary ChinaClear notice citation remains preserved for chronology.
